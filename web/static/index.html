Where leftover money goes

Each month, what's left after spending and minimum debt payments goes through these steps, in order. Income you send straight to a pot (on its item) skips them.

    Savings pots

    Savings are never spent to cover a short month unless you plan it. To send a sale or bonus straight into a pot, or pay a cost from one, open that item and pick the pot.

    Transfers from savings

    Money you move out of a pot into a month, for example to cover a short month. It shows as “from savings”, never as income.
